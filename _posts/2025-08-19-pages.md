## Create a website using GitHub Pages to showcase your bio, CV and other relevant personal, academic and project info!
*Note: This is my ‘I’m not a software engineer or web designer’ approach :)*

### Step 1

First, make sure you have signed up for a free [GitHub account][signup].

[signup]: https://github.com/signup?ref_cta=Sign+up&ref_loc=header+logged+out&ref_page=%2F&source=header-home

### Step 2

Next, we need to take a detour to install [Jekyll][jekyll-install] (for nicer looking web pages).

[jekyll-install]: https://jekyllrb.com/docs/installation/

### Step 3

Check out [this site][themes] to select a Jekyll theme (I chose a free one).

[themes]: https://jekyllthemes.io/

Once you have selected a theme, fork it to your repository and change the repository name to your username followed by '.github.io'

For example: if your username is mapwiz, change the repository name to ‘mapwiz.github.io’

### Step 4

Once the template has been forked, click ‘Settings’ then ‘Pages’ and change the ‘Branch’ settings from 'None' to 'master' and click save. After a couple of minutes, you will see a button to visit your new webpage.

### Step 5

From here, you will start your customization! Start by opening the '_config.yml' file. Here, you can link to files, add new tabs, add and modify icons and link them to external sites such as your linkedin profile or email address, and more!

*Note: I find markdown to be easier than html, so my site is comprised primarily of pages ending in ‘.md’*
