Things I’ve learned the hard way, in no particular order
- The ability to ‘do it all’ does not exist, not by anyone, not even you (yes you, dear reader)
- Sometimes you have to say no to really good and exciting opportunities to make space for the right ones
- Doing a PhD is the pursuit of expanding the field just a little bit, by answering a question (preferably one that is useful)
- Doing anything worthwhile is typically, slow, not linear, and not sexy...two steps forward, one step backward as my incredibly wise advisor likes to say
- Take a day off every once in a while
- Ask yourself, how can I make this feel easy? How can I make this enjoyable? (shoutout to James Clear for this one)
- Leave something on the table (energy wise) so you’re excited to come back to it tomorrow 
- Consistency > intensity (I like to aim for two solid focused hours of work most days...and many times I roll right past this cutoff)
