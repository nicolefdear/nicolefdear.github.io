Tips for graduate student teaching
- You don’t have to know all the answers! I have learned so much from my students each semester. Part of that involved being honest when I didn’t know the answer and letting them know I’d have to look it up or ask someone and get back to them.
- Be flexible but honor your boundaries. Providing in-person and remote options for office hours seemed to go a long way to accommodate students outside of my department or those who got sick during the semester. However, I did not respond to questions over email immediately or outside of working hours.
- Be kind. Every student has a life outside of the classroom, whether it be another job, caring for kids or being far from family and friends , everyone has other things on their plate. Giving everyone the benefit of the doubt and asking how best I could support my students if they were missing class or deadlines felt like the most important to do.
