Tips and lessons for success as a graduate student instructor
- You don’t have to know all the answers! I am always learning from and with my students. I like to be honest when I don’t know an answer and let them know I will look into it and get back to them over email or in the next session with a better response and/or additional resources.
- Be flexible but honor your boundaries. Providing in-person and remote options for office hours has always gone a long way in my experience to accommodate students outside of my department or those who got sick during the semester. Things I do not do, however, are make myself available at all hours and respond to emails outside of working hours. In my experience, clear communcation around how long a response will take (typically <24 hours) is helpful.
- Be kind. Every student has a life outside of the classroom, whether it be another job, caring for kids, or being far from family and friends. If I identified a student was missing classroom time or coursework, my approach was to reach out to the student over email and start a conversation around what they were struggling with and how I could support them - giving students the benefit of the doubt and working as a team to develop a plan together to move their learning forward.
